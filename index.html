Buy from Amazon & Flipkart in Nepal – Cash on Delivery

myCart helps you buy products from Amazon, Flipkart and other Indian stores and delivers them to your door in Nepal. You can also order from our own online store.

How it works

  1. Paste the product link from Amazon, Flipkart or another Indian store.
  2. Enter your name, phone number and delivery address in Nepal.
  3. We confirm the price in NPR and place your order.
  4. Pay with Cash on Delivery when the product reaches you.
